# Day one with Baytna Kitchen

**Ask from you:** about 5 minutes today, 2 minutes a week, and a few seconds a day.

## Today (5 minutes, together if you can)

1. **Settings ⚙**: check the shopping day (Saturday) and the fresh top-up day (Wednesday). Then set "Our usual week": which nights you cook, the leftovers night, and the nights you're usually at the parents' (*Away*). Add any allergies (strict) and ingredients you avoid.
2. **Shop → Staples**: tap Have / Low / Out on the basics you can see from where you're standing. Skip anything you're unsure of; "Not sure" is fine and is never treated as "Have".
3. **Recipes**: open a few dishes you already love and tap **Love**. Tap **Never again** on anything you won't eat. The weekly plan uses these. Add your family's dishes with **Add our own recipe** (paste ingredients one per line).

## Every week (2 minutes)

- From Thursday, **Today** shows "Next week isn't planned yet". Tap it, mark any *Away* nights, then tap **Draft this week**.
- Don't like a night? Tap **Swap** and pick one of three ideas, or search the recipes.
- Tap **Looks good · make the shopping list**. The list is ready for whoever shops.

## At the shop

- The list is grouped by where you buy things: khodarji, butcher, dairy, bakery, pantry, freezer.
- Tick items as they go in the basket. The other phone sees it immediately, and ticked items count as "Have" at home.
- "**Check amount**" means you have some at home but we don't know how much.
- ❄ "**Freeze when you get home**" appears on chicken or meat that is cooked later in the week. The app reminds you the evening before to move it to the fridge.
- **⋯ → I'll get it** lets either of you pick up items on the way home. **Share list** sends it to WhatsApp, for example to a grocer.
- The **Fresh top-up** tab holds herbs and salad for the second half of the week, so they're fresh when you cook.

## Each evening (10 seconds)

- **Today** shows tonight's dinner and what's missing. Tap **Start cooking** for the ingredients and steps (the screen stays on).
- If plans change, use **Other ideas** or **Too tired · 15 min**. Picking one replaces tonight and updates the list.
- When you finish, tap **Love / Okay / Never again** and say whether there are leftovers. Leftovers show on Today with how many days are left (3 days in the fridge by default).

That's it. If something feels like a chore after two weeks, tell me and we'll cut it.
